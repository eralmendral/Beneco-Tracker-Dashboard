How to read this viewContext for responsible interpretation
The watch score is comparative. Each unscheduled event contributes 2 points, every 12 interrupted hours contributes 1 point, an ongoing event contributes 2 points, and a feeder-matched social complaint contributes 0.5 point. Scores are normalized to the highest observed feeder. Weather, vegetation, NGCP supply, and equipment faults may all affect the result. Social comments are customer reports, not verified outage records; use the linked source before making operational decisions.
